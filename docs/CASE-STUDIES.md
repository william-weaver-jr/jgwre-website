# Case Studies — documented negotiation outcomes

These are the site's primary conversion assets. **Client permission obtained.** Names and
addresses stay omitted regardless.

Source: her own Instagram post, plus the resale deal already published on
mattstoneteam.com/jasmine-garcia.

---

## The framing

Do not present these as three wins. Present them as **three different shapes of win** —
which is exactly the argument her caption makes:

> Every house is different. Every seller is different. That's why every negotiation
> should be different.

Three deals, three completely different levers pulled. That is the proof that negotiation
isn't one skill applied repeatedly; it's reading which lever exists in *this* transaction.

## Case 2 — Resale: zero dollars off price, all value in condition

Negotiated: a **brand new roof**, **HVAC servicing**, a **home warranty**, and the
**refrigerator included**.

**This is the most persuasive of the three and should not be buried.** The reader's
reaction is the entire point: *a roof? You can just ask for a roof?* That is the moment
the page earns a lead.

Resist leading with the biggest dollar figure. Lead with the most surprising one. Case 2
demonstrates the USP better than Case 1 proves it, because it contains zero price
reduction — every dollar of value came from something the buyer didn't know to ask for.

**Use:** homepage (lead position), `/negotiation` landing page, lead magnet.

## Hard rules for presenting these

- **Never** frame as what she *will* do. Only what happened. Banned: "I'll get you,"
  "I always," "guaranteed," "every client saves."
- **Never** aggregate into an implied average ("clients save an average of…"). Three deals
  is not a data set.
- No names, no addresses, no identifying property details, no photos of the homes.
- Do not round up or restate the figures. $22,210 stays $22,210.
- Do not add a fourth case study without fresh written client permission and BIC review.
- Keep the presentation plain. These numbers do not need design amplification — oversized
  animated counters undercut the credibility they're meant to build.
