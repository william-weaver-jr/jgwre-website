 Belmont, North Carolina is a charming small town located just west of Charlotte, offering a unique blend of historic character, scenic beauty, and modern convenience. Known for its vibrant downtown district, Belmont features locally owned shops, restaurants, breweries, and community events that create a warm and welcoming atmosphere for residents and visitors alike. The area’s proximity to the Catawba River and Lake Wylie also provides plenty of opportunities for outdoor recreation, including boating, fishing, kayaking, and waterfront dining.
 Belmont’s growing real estate market includes everything from historic homes near downtown to newly built communities with modern amenities. Residents enjoy a quieter suburban lifestyle while still having easy access to Uptown Charlotte, Charlotte Douglas International Airport, and major employment centers. With its strong sense of community, walkable downtown, and attractive location, Belmont continues to be one of the most desirable places to live in the greater Charlotte area.

 Belmont continues to attract buyers who want a small-town setting with convenient access to Charlotte, and that demand has supported steady price growth in recent years. Many homes in the area trade in the mid-$300,000s to mid-$500,000s, with pricing varying by age, condition, and proximity to downtown or the water. Buyers can expect a mix of historic mill houses, brick bungalows, ranch homes, and newer craftsman-style properties.
 For sellers, thoughtful pricing and listing preparation matter in a market where presentation can influence both activity and final terms. Jasmine Garcia brings a strong educational approach, local market guidance, and seller strategy experience to help clients evaluate value, timing, and next steps in Belmont.
