 Tega Cay is a scenic lakeside community known for its resort-style atmosphere and active outdoor lifestyle. Situated along Lake Wylie, the area features beautiful homes, water views, golf, trails, and recreational amenities that make everyday living feel like a getaway. Residents enjoy a close-knit environment, quality schools, and easy access to Charlotte, making Tega Cay a standout choice for comfort and convenience.
