 JASMINE GARCIA 

 Jasmine Garcia didn’t leave teaching — she simply brought her classroom skills into the world of real estate. Known for her calm presence, clear communication, and “heart of a teacher,” Jasmine has become a trusted guide for Charlotte-area home buyers and sellers who want to feel informed, confident, and supported.
 Based in Southwest Charlotte’s Ayrsley neighborhood, Jasmine understands the local market from the inside out — from preparing listings to command top-dollar, to guiding nervous first-time buyers through the entire purchase journey. She’s worked with clients across the metro, from Ballantyne to South End, and believes real estate success starts with knowledge and trust.
 As co-owner of Vitality Homebuyers, Jasmine also brings an investor’s mindset to every transaction — offering her clients honest insight into pricing, repairs, ROI, and long-term value. Whether you’re buying, selling, or simply exploring your options, Jasmine is here to help you move forward — with confidence.
